How it works

Six steps. One connected system. No loose ends.

Every step feeds the next. We don't hand you a list of things to try — we build one engine that runs start to finish.

  1. 01

    Fix the foundation first

    Wrong positioning kills everything downstream. Before we build anything, we nail your offer, your message, and exactly who it's for. Most "marketing problems" are positioning problems in disguise.

  2. 02

    Build visibility where it counts

    Founder-led content built on the platforms your buyers actually use — Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, LinkedIn. Not all five. The right ones for you, done properly.

  3. 03

    Capture the interest you create

    Attention without a capture system is just noise. We build the landing pages, lead magnets and booking flows that turn viewers into real enquiries.

  4. 04

    Respond before they go cold

    The average business takes 47 hours to respond to a lead. We set up automation that responds in minutes — because the first reply wins most of the time.

  5. 05

    Nurture until they're ready

    Most leads need 5–7 touches before they buy. We build the email and WhatsApp sequences that keep warming them — so nothing leaks out of the bottom of your pipeline.

  6. 06

    Hand you a ready-to-close calendar

    A polished proposal and a booked call, delivered to you. The prospect already trusts you before the conversation starts. Your job is just to close.

One rule

We build everything up to the sale. We don't close for you — that relationship belongs to you. What we hand over is a calendar of warm, qualified prospects who already want to say yes. You finish the conversation.

Got questions?

We've heard them all. Here are the real answers.

What makes this different from hiring a social media manager?

A social media manager posts content. We build a system — positioning, content, funnels, automation, nurture and proposals — that connects every piece to the next and ends with a warm, ready-to-buy prospect on your calendar. Content without a system is just noise with good lighting.

Do I need to already be making money or have an audience?

No. Kaziranga Greens Football Club started with no significant online presence and zero ad spend. 1,000+ leads and 80+ admissions in 90 days. The system works from a standing start — because it's built on positioning and organic reach, not an existing audience or a paid budget.

I'm not good on camera. Will this still work?

Yes. Founder-led content doesn't mean you have to be a presenter. We build the strategy around how you're actually comfortable showing up. Some of our best-performing content is low-production and conversational. We'll find your version of it.

Which package is right for me?

Start with DIY if you want to test the system for free before committing. Move to DWY if you want us building it alongside you — this is where most clients get the fastest results. Choose DFY if you're established and want to fully hand it over. We'll also give you a straight recommendation on the strategy call.

How quickly will I see results?

For Kaziranga Greens, enquiries started in week one. By the end of month one they were running at 50+ a day. That's not a guarantee — every business is different — but our system is built for speed, not slow burns. The 6-phase structure is specifically sequenced to get early signals fast so we can double down on what's working.

What if I've tried agencies before and got burned?

Most agencies sell outputs — posts, followers, reports. We sell a system with a specific end goal: warm prospects on your calendar. That's a different contract. We also don't take everyone who applies. If we don't genuinely think we can move the needle for your business, we say so on the strategy call before anyone spends anything.

What does your guarantee actually mean?

On DWY and DFY: if you follow the system, show up to calls, give us access to what we need — and we haven't moved the needle — we keep working until we do. No extra charge. No time limit. We've never had to invoke it. The strategy call filters for the businesses where we're confident we can deliver. If that's not you, we won't take your money.

Do we need an ad budget?

No. The entire foundation is built on organic reach. Kaziranga Greens: zero ad spend, 1,000+ leads. Ads can be added later when you want to pour fuel on a system that's already working — but they're never the starting point.

Do we need a website to get started?

Not on day one. Our clients start booking enquiries using just their social profiles, a landing page and the right outreach system — no full website required. When you do need one, we build it as part of the system: a clean, fast, conversion-focused page that does one job — turns a visitor into a booked call. Not a brochure. Not a portfolio. A tool.

You've worked with Red Bull and Bernd Zangerl — are you only for big names?

Not at all. Those collaborations came from our roots in adventure storytelling — that's where CineCompass started. We've built the same systems for early-stage academies, solo coaches and small adventure operators. You don't need to be a Red Bull athlete to get a system built like one was made for you.
